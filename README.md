# kmap
Website for Kathryn Maple

This is the website for the artist Kathryn Maple

The content of the website should include

## Better works
10 to 15 pictures of her best work (possibly in rotation)
## Exibitions
Past, current and future exhibitions
## Contact
Email contact for interested buyers of galery curators
## Biography
Bio of Kathryn and her work




## Inspiration
https://shop.a1043.com/
Great site presumably made by cargo, the idea of the hovering url links is quite stylish

https://lyndseyingram.com/
A site that kathyrn likes, its a stylish and well presented site showing images in large thumbnail format, clicking on them gives a full size description of the picture in its dedicated page
https://lyndseyingram.com/exhibitions/75-the-poetry-of-objects-atelier-fornasetti-upcoming-exhibition/works/ this page could actually be a good starting point for the better works section
https://lyndseyingram.com/exhibitions/75/works/artworks-8919-fornasetti-umbrella-stand-rovine-1950s-re-edition/ is a good example of how to showcase an art piece on its on page.  It contains details on the art work, an enquire button, a share button and also a further images section that shows you the art work in different settings within a room.  This could eventually lead onto the idea that Kathryn had that she wanted the functionality to showcase an art work,  

https://www.victoria-miro.com/
Another site that Kathryn likes.  Different layout style from lyndseyingram but still very effective.  The way the exibitions are presented is quite tidy.  The buttons for connecting to socials is done very stylish here.

The winning format is this for starters 
https://lyndseyingram.com/exhibitions/75-the-poetry-of-objects-atelier-fornasetti-upcoming-exhibition/works/
The page similar to that will also be the lead site.  The other pages will be seperate pages (so its not all one big scrolly rolly type thing)
